Diego’s Drive Tools is a personal OAuth application used with rclone for Google Drive file operations. These terms describe that utility and its informational website.

Authorized use

Use only accounts and data you own or are authorized to access. Follow Google’s applicable terms and the terms of any third-party tools or destinations you use. The website does not offer public account registration or a hosted transfer service.

File operations

Transfers and synchronization commands can overwrite or delete files. You are responsible for reviewing the command, source, destination, and permissions, and for keeping suitable backups. Do not expose credentials in public repositories or support requests.

Availability

The utility is provided as available, without a service-level commitment. Its behavior and availability depend on rclone, Google APIs, network access, and your local configuration. Nothing here limits rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law.

Ending access

You may stop using the utility and revoke its Google access at any time. Removing access does not automatically remove previously transferred files. Read the privacy policy for data handling details.
